Income tax rates and slabs in Bangladesh (2026-27)

Assessment year 2026-27, for income earned 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. Last checked 25 November 2026.

Bangladesh taxes individuals in slabs: the first part of your income is tax-free, and each slab above it is taxed at a higher rate, from 10% up to 30%. Here are this year's limits and rates, from NBR's Income Tax Paripatra 2026-2027, with worked examples.

Work out your own tax

The tax-free limit

The first slab of income is taxed at 0%. How big it is depends on who you are:

TaxpayerTax-free limit
Most taxpayers৳4,00,000
Women, and anyone aged 65 or over৳4,50,000
People with a disability৳5,25,000
Third-gender taxpayers৳5,25,000
Gazetted war-wounded freedom fighters and gazetted wounded July fighters৳5,50,000

A parent or guardian of a person with a disability gets another ৳50,000 for each such child or dependant. If both parents are taxpayers, only one of them can claim it.

The tax slabs

After the tax-free limit, your income is taxed slab by slab:

SlabRate
First ৳4,00,000 (or your tax-free limit above)0%
Next ৳3,00,00010%
Next ৳4,00,00015%
Next ৳5,00,00020%
Next ৳20,00,00025%
Everything above that30%

For most taxpayers, that means 10% on income from ৳4 lakh to ৳7 lakh, 15% up to ৳11 lakh, 20% up to ৳16 lakh, 25% up to ৳36 lakh, and 30% above ৳36 lakh. With a higher tax-free limit, each slab starts later by the same amount.

Only the income inside each slab is taxed at that slab's rate. Moving into a higher slab never makes your tax on the lower slabs go up.

Worked examples

A salaried man earning ৳12,00,000 a year

One third of salary is tax-free, up to ৳5,00,000. One third of ৳12,00,000 is ৳4,00,000, so his taxable income is ৳8,00,000.

First ৳4,00,000 at 0%৳0
Next ৳3,00,000 at 10%৳30,000
Last ৳1,00,000 at 15%৳15,000
Tax before any rebate৳45,000

A woman with the same salary has a ৳4,50,000 tax-free limit, so she pays 10% on ৳3,00,000 (৳30,000) and 15% on the last ৳50,000 (৳7,500): ৳37,500 in all.

Both can cut this with the investment tax rebate, and any tax already deducted from their salary is subtracted from what's left.

Minimum tax

If your income is above your tax-free limit, you pay at least ৳5,000, or ৳1,000 if this is your first return. This applies even if the slab tax is lower, or the investment rebate brings it below that.

For example, an income of ৳4,20,000 gives slab tax of ৳2,000 (10% of ৳20,000), but the tax payable is the ৳5,000 minimum.

Wealth surcharge

People with high net wealth pay a surcharge on top of their income tax. It is a percentage of the income tax, not of the wealth:

Net wealthSurcharge
Up to ৳4 croreNone
Over ৳4 crore, up to ৳10 crore10%
Over ৳10 crore, up to ৳20 crore20%
Over ৳20 crore, up to ৳50 crore30%
Over ৳50 crore35%

The 10% surcharge also applies below ৳4 crore if you own more than one car, or house property with a total floor area over 8,000 square feet.

From slab tax to what you pay

  1. Add up your income from salary, rent, business, interest, dividends and Sanchaypatra, leaving out tax-free income such as one third of salary and money sent home through banks.
  2. Apply the slabs above to get your tax.
  3. Subtract the investment rebate.
  4. If the result is below the minimum tax, pay the minimum instead.
  5. Add any wealth surcharge.
  6. Subtract tax already paid: tax deducted from salary, bank interest and Sanchaypatra, and any advance tax. What's left is what you pay with your return, or a refund if it's negative.
