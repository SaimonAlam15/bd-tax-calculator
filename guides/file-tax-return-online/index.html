How to file your income tax return online in Bangladesh (2026-27)

Assessment year 2026-27, for income earned 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. Last checked 25 November 2026.

For 2026-27, most individual taxpayers must file their return on NBR's e-return site, etaxnbr.gov.bd. The whole process happens online: you register, fill in the return, pay any tax due, and download your acknowledgement slip. There is nothing to take to a bank or a tax office.

Deadline: 30 November 2026. NBR often extends it, so check the news before relying on that.

Work out your tax first with the calculator. Its "File your return online" section shows your own figures for each screen below, with copy buttons.

1. Who has to file online

NBR has made the e-return mandatory for individual taxpayers again this year. These people may still file a paper return at their tax circle:

  • people aged 65 or older
  • people with a disability
  • Bangladeshis living abroad
  • foreign nationals working in Bangladesh
  • legal representatives filing for a taxpayer who has died

Anyone else who cannot register on the e-return because of a technical problem can ask their Additional or Joint Commissioner of Taxes for approval to file on paper.

2. Documents to have ready

You don't upload any documents to the e-return, but you need the figures from them, and you should keep them in case the tax office asks.

  • Your e-TIN certificate and National ID card
  • Last year's return or acknowledgement slip, for last year's net wealth
  • Salary certificate from your employer, showing the tax deducted
  • Interest and tax deduction certificates from each bank, and for Sanchaypatra
  • Dividend statements, rent records, or business accounts, if you have that income
  • Proof of investments for the tax rebate: DPS statements, insurance premium receipts, provident fund statements, share purchases
  • Encashment certificates or bank statements for money received from abroad
  • Challans or certificates for advance tax, such as the tax paid when renewing a car's registration
  • Your balances on 30 June 2026: bank accounts, cash, investments, property and loans

3. Register on the e-return site

  1. Go to etaxnbr.gov.bd and choose Register.
  2. Enter your 12-digit TIN and a mobile number registered in your own name with your NID.
  3. Enter the code sent by SMS, then set a password.
  4. Log in with your TIN and password.

Common problems

  • The mobile number isn't accepted. It must be biometrically registered to your own NID, not a family member's. Your mobile operator can check this.
  • You don't have a TIN. Get an e-TIN first at secure.incometax.gov.bd. It is free and takes a few minutes.
  • You forgot your password. Use Forgot Password on the login page; a reset code is sent to your mobile.

NBR's call centre can help with registration: 09643717171.

Once you're logged in, go to Submission → Regular e-Return. The return is a series of tabs, covered below. Labels in quotes are the portal's own wording.

4. Assessment screen

  • "Resident Status": Resident, if you were in Bangladesh for 183 days or more in the income year (or 90 days, if you were here 365 days over the previous four years).
  • "Any taxable income in the income year?": Yes if you had any income, then tick each kind you had, such as employment (salary), rent, business, and financial assets (interest, dividends, Sanchaypatra).
  • "Income Earned outside Bangladesh": leave this unticked if all you have is money sent home through banks. That is tax-free income and goes on the Income screen instead.
  • "Any income which is fully exempted from tax?": Yes if you received money from abroad, or other tax-free income.

5. IT10B requirements

Five questions decide whether you must submit the assets and liabilities statement (IT10B): net wealth over ৳50 lakh, owning a car, property abroad, being a company director, and owning house property. If all are No, the portal asks whether you want to submit it anyway. Submitting it is worth it: it keeps a record of your wealth that next year's return builds on.

6. Income screens

Each kind of income you ticked gets its own tab. Have these figures ready from your documents:

IncomeWhat you'll need
SalaryEach part of your salary for the year (basic, allowances, bonuses), and the tax your employer deducted
RentRent received for each property, municipal tax and land revenue paid, loan interest, and any tax deducted by the tenant
BusinessNet profit for the year, and any tax deducted or collected at source
Financial assetsInterest, dividends and Sanchaypatra profit before tax, and the tax deducted from each

Money sent from abroad goes on the "Tax Exempted Income" tab. Choose "Foreign Remittance" and enter the "Total Remitted Amount", with "Mode of Remittance" set to Banking Channel. The portal also asks for the country, the source of the income, your employer, the bank, and the receiving account's name and number.

Sanchaypatra changed this year. From 2026-27, the tax deducted from Sanchaypatra profit is advance tax, not final tax. The profit is added to your income and taxed at the normal rates, the tax already deducted is subtracted, and any excess can be refunded.

7. Expenditure (IT10BB)

This tab asks for your household spending for the year: food and clothing, housing, transport, utilities, education, festivals and other costs. "Tax, Charges, Etc. Paid" fills itself in. The total becomes your "Annual Living Expense" on the next tab, so estimate it honestly from your bank statements.

8. Assets and liabilities (IT10B)

List what you owned and owed on 30 June 2026. If you filed online last year, "Import and Autofill" copies last year's statement; then update every value to 30 June 2026.

The Summary must show a Difference of 0. The portal checks that your wealth adds up: this year's net wealth, minus last year's, plus your living expenses and other outflows, must equal your income, tax-free income and other receipts. If the Difference isn't 0, something is missing, usually a bank balance, cash in hand, a loan, or a gift you received.

9. Tax and payment

The portal works out your tax itself: total income, gross tax, the investment rebate, minimum tax, any surcharge, and the total amount payable. Check these against the calculator's results. If a figure differs, go back to the tab it comes from.

Click "Update Tax Payment Status" to add tax already paid: tax deducted at source (salary, bank interest, Sanchaypatra) and any advance tax. The portal then shows:

  • "Incentive (For 1st quarter submission)": the early-filing rebate, only for returns filed by 30 September
  • "Refundable": tax you paid beyond what you owe
  • "Net Payable": what you still need to pay

10. Pay and submit

If "Net Payable" is more than zero, click "Pay Now" and pay on the payment page that opens. You don't need to visit a bank. If you have nothing to pay, "Pay Now" stays greyed out. If you're due a refund, NBR checks the claim before paying it.

Then click "Proceed to online return", review the whole return, and submit it. Your return is only complete once it is paid and submitted.

11. After you file

Download the acknowledgement slip and a copy of the return from the portal. The slip is your proof of filing; banks, employers and government offices often ask for it. You don't need to send anything to your tax circle.

Keep your documents and the calculator's spreadsheet too. Next year's return starts from this year's net wealth, and "Import and Autofill" copies this year's statement.

12. Filing late

Under the 2026-27 rules, when you file changes what you pay:

FiledEffect
1 July to 30 September 20265% rebate on the tax payable, up to ৳25,000
1 October to 31 December 2026No rebate and no extra tax
1 January to 31 March 2027Extra tax of 2% of the tax payable, at least ৳3,000
1 April to 30 June 2027Extra tax of 5% of the tax payable, at least ৳5,000

The deadline is still 30 November, and filing after it can bring other consequences, so file on time if you can.

13. Common questions

Do I have to pay my tax at a bank?

No. If you file online, you pay through "Pay Now" on the e-return. Paying at a bank with a challan is for paper returns.

Do I need to upload my documents?

No. You enter the figures only. Keep the documents for at least a few years in case the tax office asks for them.

My income is below the tax-free limit. Do I still file?

You may still have to, for example if you filed a return in any of the past three years, and many banks and offices ask for proof of filing. The tax-free limit this year is ৳4 lakh, ৳4.5 lakh for women and people aged 65 or over, and higher for people with a disability and some others.

Is there minimum tax?

Yes. If your income is above the tax-free limit, you pay at least ৳5,000, or ৳1,000 if you're filing for the first time, even if the rebate brings your tax lower.

What if the portal's figures differ from the calculator's?

The e-return's figures are the official ones. Check which tab the difference comes from; it is usually an income line, a deduction or an investment entered differently. If you think the calculator is wrong, please let us know.
