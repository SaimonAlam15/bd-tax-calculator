About this calculator

A free income tax calculator for individual taxpayers in Bangladesh, in English and Bangla. It works out the tax on your income for assessment year 2026-27 (income earned from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026) and walks you through filing the return on NBR's e-return site.

Calculate your tax

What it covers

  • Salary, including the tax-free third of salary (up to ৳5 lakh)
  • House rent, with the repairs allowance and other allowed deductions
  • Business or professional income
  • Bank and FDR interest, dividends and Sanchaypatra profit, with the tax already deducted
  • Money sent home from abroad through banks, which is tax-free
  • The investment tax rebate on DPS, Sanchaypatra, life insurance, provident fund, shares and more
  • Higher tax-free limits for women, people aged 65 or over, people with a disability and others
  • Minimum tax, the wealth surcharge, and the rebate or extra tax for filing early or late
  • Tax already paid, giving the final amount to pay or the refund due

It shows each step: income by head, tax slab by slab, and which limit decided your rebate. You can download the whole calculation as a spreadsheet, and the filing guide shows which figure goes in which box of the e-return.

What it doesn't cover

Companies, firms and non-residents; capital gains; agricultural income; income kept abroad; and turnover tax. If any of these apply to you, use the calculator as a starting point only and check with a tax adviser.

Where the rules come from

The tax rates, limits and dates follow the Income Tax Act 2023 and this year's changes to it, as set out in NBR's own guide, Income Tax Paripatra 2026-2027 (2 September 2026). The rules were last checked against it on 29 October 2026.

The calculation is checked by automated tests, including the worked examples in the Paripatra itself, and covering the tax-free limits, minimum tax, the rebate limits, the wealth surcharge, refunds and the filing-date rules. The tests run before every update goes live.

How it's kept up to date

Bangladesh's tax rules change every year with the budget in June, and NBR publishes the year's Paripatra a few months later. The calculator is updated for each new tax year once those are out. The date the rules were last checked is shown at the bottom of the calculator.

Your privacy

Everything you enter stays in your browser and is deleted 3 hours after your last change. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. The site shows Google ads to cover its costs. See the privacy policy for details.
